# A Royal Ball

King Richard sat upon his golden chair in that grand hall. Lords and ladies danced all night long. Music from a small band rang out, drums and horns making joyful sounds. A young woman in a blue gown caught his stare. She was stunning, fair and tall.

They danced until dawn broke. Morning sunlight poured in through tall glass. He asked for her hand in marriage right away. She said yes with a laugh. Soon they had a child, a tiny boy. They lived in joy, all days long, in that royal mansion by tall walls and vast lands.
