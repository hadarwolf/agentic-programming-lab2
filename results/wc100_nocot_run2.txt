# A Trail Through Dark Mountains

Sam and Dan built a cabin by a cold, distant mountain path. No roads or towns lay within sight. Isolation brought them calm days.

Both men cut wood, hunted, and fished in icy streams. Nights brought stars so vivid and bright, paintings of cosmic glory hung across dark skies.

Autumn came with gold and rust colors coating all brush and tall oaks. Winters brought snow piling high on roof and ground.

Years passed. This far outpost grew into sanctuary for both companions. Away from chaos and human confusion, they found what they sought: tranquility, friendship, and simplicity among wild, untamed country.
