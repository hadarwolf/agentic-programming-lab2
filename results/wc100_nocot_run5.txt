A dark night falls upon a small town. Two cats roam through shadows, hunting for food. Their silent paws touch cold ground. Suddenly, a loud sound frightens both animals. They run frantically down a narrow alley. A rat scurries past them, quick and nimble. Both cats miss their quarry, jumping at nothing but air. Frustrated, they stop to catch breath. Morning sun arrives soon, bringing light and warmth. The cats finally find milk by a barn door. They drink greedily, satisfied at last. Daylight brings calm to this wild night's chaos.
