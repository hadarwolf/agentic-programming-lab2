# Sunlit Walk

Jim and Gina took a path through tall grass on a bright day. Birds sang from oak and willow limbs. Clouds drifted slowly across a vast, blue sky. Small rocks lay scattered on dirt paths. Butterflies and moths fluttered around wild plants, dancing with careless joy. A small stream bubbled and burbled, cool and bright. Fish swam quickly in its depths. Jim and Gina sat on a log and gazed at all this natural splendor without a worry or anxiety. Hours drifted past. This was a day of unusual calm and bliss, a gift from nature's hand.
