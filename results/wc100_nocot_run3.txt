# A Sunny Day

Taj sat by a calm pond, watching fish swim in lazy circles. A small dog ran past, tail up high. Birds sang songs in tall oak and ash bushes. Clouds drifted through a bright, warm sky. An old man cast his fishing rod far into cool, dark water. Two kids played with a ball on soft grass. A woman walked by, smiling at all the joy around. Wind brought scents of blooming flowers. Taj took a long, calm sip of cold liquid. Nothing could ruin this perfect, sunny day. Tomorrow, all would start again with dawn's soft rays lighting up that same calm pond.
